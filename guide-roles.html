Roles

Overview · Members · Organizers · Restricted events · Attendance · Roles · Server admins

All of this is in /settings (needs Manage Server, unless your server changed Settings access).

Categories

/settings → Categories. A category is a name that stands for several roles, such as Healer = Cleric, Druid: any of its member roles fills a Healer slot, and reminders reach them.

  • Without a Discord role: type a new name and choose Name only. It works by name alone in slots, ping rules and Who can sign up, and anything that would ping it pings its member roles. (Create role makes a role for it instead, which can be @mentioned; Cancel is for a typo.)
  • With a role of its own: the role is automatic on Paid (Let the bot manage role: the bot gives and removes it to match members’ roles) and manual on Free (you give it, or members pick it).

Self-assignable roles

/settings → Self-assignable roles: the roles members can pick with /roles and /whoami.

  • Up to four named groups (for example Classes and Events), one menu each. A Notifications menu is added from the roles your ping rules mention (Offer ping roles turns it off); a ping role that's also in a group is listed in both unless Repeat group roles in Notifications is off.
  • Members can pick roles: Off leaves roles to staff: /roles is off and /whoami only sets nicknames and preferences.
  • The bot can only give roles below its own: a role above it stays in its group, marked ⚠️ with how to fix it, and is offered once you drag the bot’s role above it in Server Settings → Roles. Roles that grant permissions need your confirmation first (see Safety).

Role posts and button looks

  • Post role buttons keeps a post per group in the roles channel (chosen under General), with a button per role: members press it to add the role and again to remove it, and Show my … privately lists which of that post’s roles they have. Edit post texts… sets the line under each post’s heading. The posts follow the groups and switches.
  • Button looks: choose a group in the last menu, then Edit looks…. A group’s buttons get a colour (grey, blurple, green or red: the four Discord allows), and each role an emoji (Tank: 🛡️, or a server emoji as :name:), a colour of its own if wanted (Healer: 💚 green), and a name for its button in quotes (WAR: ⚔️ red "Warrior" shows ⚔️ Warrior on a red button). Emoji and names show in the /roles and /whoami menus too; a role with an emoji icon uses it until you set one.
  • Pick emoji with reactions lets you choose emoji with Discord’s own emoji picker instead: the bot opens a private thread for you in the admin channel (or the announcement channel), asks for one role at a time, and deletes the thread when you’re done.

Entry button

/settings → Entry button: a post with one button, in a channel you choose (your rules or welcome channel), that gives new members a role such as Member to let them into the server: a light alternative to a verification bot. It only adds the role (staff remove it) and is kept apart from the self-assignable roles. Choose the channel and role, edit the text and button label, and optionally apply the new-member wait from Safety. Blocked members can’t use it, and each entry is in the audit log. (Discord’s own Onboarding, on Community servers, can do this too.)

Typing role names, and unused roles

Roles can be typed by name. If a name matches no role, the form offers to create it: new roles have no permissions and go to the bottom of the role list. Unused roles… (under Self-assignable roles) lists roles the bot created that nothing uses any more, split into those nobody has and those people have, with a delete button for each (the second asks twice). If several roles share a name, it offers to remove the extra copies (the oldest is kept, and only copies with no members and no permissions are removed). A role that already exists is never created again. The bot needs Manage Roles for that, and for category roles and /roles, with its own role above the roles it manages.
