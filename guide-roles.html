All of this is in /settings (needs Manage Server, unless your server changed Settings access).

Categories

/settings → Roles → Categories. A category is a name that stands for several roles, such as Healer = Cleric, Druid: any of its member roles fills a Healer slot, and reminders reach them.

  • Without a Discord role: leave the role picker empty and give the category a name. It works by name alone in slots, pings and Who can sign up, and anything that would ping it pings its member roles. To give it a role, make the role in Discord and choose it in the form.
  • With a role of its own: it's manual unless you turn on Automatic for it (Paid): then, while Management is on, the bot gives and removes it to match members’ roles. Manual means you give it, or members pick it.

Every plan can create, edit and remove categories. If Paid expires, categories and Discord roles are kept and automatic ones turn manual; they resume when Paid is restored and Management is on. On Paid, the Inbox lists categories that could be automatic but aren't (Get the most out of your subscription); it never posts to the admin channel, and marking it read leaves them manual until another category could be automatic.

Management

Role management is on for new servers (servers that had it off keep it off). Turn it on or off in /settings → Roles → Management → Settings; /setup's Roles step and Check Role Setup check the bot's role first. EventMoogle needs Manage Roles and its role above the configured roles it assigns. Matching-only and ping-only roles do not need to move. The default invite includes Manage Roles; older installs without it get a re-invite link. Permission or hierarchy problems appear in Status and the configured admin channel.

The switch controls self-roles, automatic category roles and linked static roles. Turning it off preserves Discord roles and group data. Matching, restrictions and pings still work; static membership can still be edited in the database.

/settings → Roles → Management: the roles members can pick with /whoami.

  • Up to four named groups (for example Classes and Events), one menu each. A Notifications menu is added from the roles your presets ping (Settings → Offer Ping Roles turns it off); a ping role that's also in a group is listed in both unless Settings → Repeat Group Roles in Notifications is off.
  • Management: Off leaves roles to staff: /whoami only sets nicknames and preferences.
  • The bot can only give roles below its own: a role above it stays in its group, marked ⚠️ with how to fix it, and is offered once you drag the bot’s role above it in Server Settings → Roles. Roles that grant permissions need your confirmation first (see Safety).

Channel, role posts and button looks

Open /settings → Roles → Channel for channel selection, Add/Remove Role Posts, Edit Post Texts, Edit Looks and Pick Emojis.

  • Add Role Posts keeps a post per group in the roles channel (chosen under Roles → Channel), with a button per role: members press it to add the role and again to remove it, and Show my … privately lists which of that post’s roles they have. Edit Post Texts sets the line under each post’s heading. The posts follow the groups and switches.
  • Button looks: choose a group in the last menu, then Edit Looks. A group’s buttons get a colour (grey, blurple, green or red: the four Discord allows), and each role an emoji (Tank: 🛡️, or a server emoji as :name:), a colour of its own if wanted (Healer: 💚 green), and a name for its button in quotes (WAR: ⚔️ red "Warrior" shows ⚔️ Warrior on a red button). Emoji and names show in the /whoami menus too; a role with an emoji icon uses it until you set one.
  • Pick Emojis lets you choose emoji with Discord’s own emoji picker instead: the bot opens a private thread for you in the admin channel (or the announcement channel), asks for one role at a time, and deletes the thread when you’re done.

Entry button

The entry button was retired in v3.1. Old buttons cannot grant roles; EventMoogle removes their controls while preserving message text, embeds and attachments. Failed cleanup retries and is reported to admins. Existing Discord roles remain. Use Discord’s Onboarding for server entry.

Typing role names, and unused roles

Roles can be typed by name. If a name matches no role, the form offers to create it: new roles have no permissions and go to the bottom of the role list. Unused Roles (under Roles → Management) lists roles the bot created that nothing uses any more, split into those nobody has and those people have, with a delete button for each (the second asks twice). If several roles share a name, it offers to remove the extra copies (the oldest is kept, and only copies with no members and no permissions are removed). A role that already exists is never created again. The bot needs Manage Roles for that, and for category roles and self-assignable roles, with its own role above the roles it manages.
