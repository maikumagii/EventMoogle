Attendance (Paid)

Open /settings → Insights → Attendance for a filterable member overview, recent events, member history and CSV exports. Members affected by the attendance rule or one counted miss away appear first. Use Attendance Rules to configure the policy. Select a member and choose Edit attendance to correct a saved event, or select a recent event to edit its recorded roster. Choose Attended, Late or No-Show and press Save Attendance; deselect a member from the chosen mark to clear it. Historical records remain editable after the original event or roster is gone. Reports and corrections retain the attendance permissions and Paid requirement.

Only the server owner sees Wipe server attendance. Type WIPE to permanently delete all server attendance marks, event attendance records, voice history and recurring confirmations. This cannot be undone. Attendance rules and member exemptions are preserved; future attendance is recorded normally.

Taking attendance

  • During the event window, the bot samples roster members and the event’s creator in any of the server’s voice or stage channels, even when the event has no channel. Roster members seen in at least two samples are pre-marked attended. Other members in voice are never sampled.
  • The pre-event roster’s Attendance button (when enabled) opens a roster-only editor, during or after the event. It stays on the final roster after the event ends and the thread closes, so organizers can correct attendance later (cancelled events keep no buttons). Choose Attended, Late or No-Show, select the players receiving that mark, then press Save Attendance. Mark all Attended marks every roster player, across all pages. Unmarked players stay unmarked; interested members, standby players and earlier placements outside the posted roster do not become no-shows. Voice samples and 🕒 late-arrival suggestions remain visible. Manual choices override voice suggestions; a manual Late mark does not invent an arrival time.

History and export

  • In an event’s thread, /attendance opens that event’s Attendance panel for whoever can mark it, live or ended. Everyone else sees their own record with that event’s mark first.
  • Outside an event thread, /attendance shows ordinary members their own record: attended, half and late attendances, no-shows, your rate, and where you stand under the no-show rule.
  • /attendance opens Attendance management for members who can manage events or the server: recent events, member lookup and attendance rates.
  • In Attendance management, Export CSV downloads every attendance mark and recurring-date answer, or the selected member’s.

How attendance counts

The rules below are worked out when attendance is read, so changing a setting applies to past events too.

  • Voice-required events: put Voice: required in a Discord event’s description. Members marked attended who weren’t seen in any of the server’s voice or stage channels get half attendance (🎧): it counts as half in the rate and half a miss for the no-show rule. A Late mark without voice counts the late weight × ½ (¼ with the default), including a hand-marked Late. Turn this off in /settings → Attendance rules.
  • Late arrivals: members first seen in any of the server’s voice or stage channels more than 10 minutes after its scheduled start are late (🕒) and count ½ in the rate and the no-show rule (half a miss). Lateness counts from the later of the scheduled start and the creator’s first voice sample. A creator never seen leaves it on the scheduled start. The Attendance panel and summary note a late leader; the creator’s own mark follows the usual rules. A rescheduled event counts from its new time; starting early doesn’t make anyone late. /settings → Attendance rules → Late arrivals… changes the minutes (0 turns it off) and whether late counts in full, ¾ or ½.
  • Voiding an event: if an event’s attendance shouldn’t count (a test run, or one padded with attendance), /settings → Attendance rules → Void an event… voids it. It stays listed, marked ⊘, but no longer counts toward rates or the no-show rule, including its unanswered recurring-date confirmations; choose it again to restore it. Both are recorded in the audit log.
  • Count only confirmed attendance (on by default): an event counts once its creator or an administrator confirms its attendance with Save Attendance. Until then its marks stay listed as not confirmed (⏳) and don’t count toward rates or the no-show rule; the event’s own attendance panel still shows them, ready to confirm. Turn it off in /settings → Attendance rules to count every recorded event. It applies to past events too.

Attendance with extra characters

When a member brings extra characters, the attendance editor lists each character separately. Voice samples belong to the member and inform each character line, including the any-channel and late-leader rules. The no-show rule counts the member once per event: if one character attended, another character’s No-Show does not make that event a miss. Only all-No-Show lines make a missed event. Marks for temporarily hidden extras stay stored when the host’s plan or Characters on events switch changes.

The v3.4.0 upgrade moves every saved ¾ late weight to ½; saved full and ½ choices stay. The current settings apply to past events too. Unanswered recurring-date confirmations still count as misses when Count only confirmed attendance is on.

The no-show rule

Off by default (/settings → Attendance rules). Members who missed more than a set share of their recent signups (no-shows in attendance, or not confirming a recurring date) wait on standby first, behind members who chose standby, and are called in at the standby fill time if places remain. Saying Can't make it in time isn't a miss, and the window moves on as members attend. /attendance shows where you stand; members who can manage the server can select a member in Attendance management and change their exemption.
