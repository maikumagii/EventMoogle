Attendance (Paid or an event pass for one date)

Open /settings → Insights → Attendance for a filterable member overview, recent events, member history and CSV exports. Members affected by the attendance rule or one counted miss away appear first. Use Attendance Rules to choose how attendance counts. Select a member and choose Edit attendance to correct a saved event, or select a recent event to edit its recorded roster. Choose Attended, Late or No-Show; changes save as you make them. Press Save Attendance when you’re done; deselect a member from the chosen mark to clear it. Historical records remain editable after the original event or roster is gone. Reports and corrections retain the attendance permissions and Paid requirement.

Only the server owner sees Wipe server attendance. Type WIPE to permanently delete all server attendance marks, event attendance records, voice history and recurring confirmations. This cannot be undone. Attendance rules and member exemptions are preserved; future attendance is recorded normally.

Taking attendance

  • During the event window, the bot samples roster members and the event’s creator in any of the server’s voice or stage channels, even when the event has no channel. Roster members seen in at least two samples are pre-marked attended. Other members in voice are never sampled.
  • The pre-event roster’s Attendance button (when enabled) opens a roster-only editor, during or after the event. It stays on the final roster after the event ends and the thread closes, so organizers can correct attendance later (cancelled events keep no buttons). Choose Attended, Late or No-Show, select the players receiving that mark. Changes save as you make them, including Mark all Attended. Press Save Attendance when you’re done to confirm the event; if you don’t, it is confirmed when the event ends if someone marked it. After confirmation, changes save and count as you make them. Mark all Attended marks every roster player, across all pages. Unmarked players stay unmarked; interested members, standby players and earlier placements outside the posted roster do not become no-shows. Voice samples and 🕒 late-arrival suggestions remain visible. Manual choices override voice suggestions; a manual Late mark does not invent an arrival time.
  • Add someone… adds a person who took part but wasn’t on the roster: a member who didn’t sign up, a saved guest, or a new guest (someone not on Discord). They start unmarked and stay on that event’s attendance.

History and export

  • In an event’s thread, /attendance opens that event’s Attendance panel for whoever can mark it, live or ended. Everyone else sees their own record with that event’s mark first.
  • Outside an event thread, /attendance shows ordinary members their own record: attended, half and late attendances, no-shows, your score, and where you stand under the no-show rule.
  • /attendance opens Attendance management for members who can manage events or the server: recent events, member lookup and attendance scores.
  • In Attendance management, Export CSV downloads every attendance mark and recurring-date answer, or the selected member’s.
  • Guests lists the people not on Discord that organizers added. Choose one to see their record, link them to their account once they join Discord (their attendance moves over; an existing mark for the member on the same event wins), or delete them with everything recorded about them. Guests are left out of the no-show rule.

How attendance counts

Attendance reports show what a member attended: Aldric — 6/7 attended · Within rule. Late and half attendance count partly, so a score can read 6.5/7 attended; partial scores show one decimal, without a percentage. A member’s record says Recent signups: attended 6.5 of the last 7. The score uses the same counted signups and weights as the no-show rule.

The rules below are worked out when attendance is read, so changing a setting applies to past events too.

  • Voice-required events: put Voice: required in a Discord event’s description. Members pre-marked from voice samples who weren’t seen in any of the server’s voice or stage channels get half attendance (🎧): it counts as half in the rate and half a miss for the no-show rule. By hand, choose Attended, not in voice for the same half; a hand-marked Attended counts in full, whatever the samples say. A Late mark without voice counts the late weight × ½ (¼ with the default), including a hand-marked Late. Turn this off in /settings → Attendance rules.
  • Late arrivals: members first seen in any of the server’s voice or stage channels more than 10 minutes after its scheduled start are late (🕒) and count ½ in the rate and the no-show rule (half a miss). Lateness counts from the later of the scheduled start and the creator’s first voice sample. A creator never seen leaves it on the scheduled start. The Attendance panel and summary note a late leader; the creator’s own mark follows the usual rules. A rescheduled event counts from its new time; starting early doesn’t make anyone late. /settings → Attendance rules → Late arrivals… changes the minutes (0 turns it off) and whether late counts in full, ¾ or ½.
  • Voiding an event: if an event’s attendance shouldn’t count (a test run, or one padded with attendance), /settings → Attendance rules → Void an event… voids it. It stays listed, marked ⊘, but no longer counts toward rates or the no-show rule, including its unanswered recurring-date confirmations; choose it again to restore it. Both are recorded in the audit log.
  • Count only confirmed attendance (on by default): an event counts once an organizer saves its attendance with Save Attendance, or when it ends if someone marked it. An event nobody marked stays unconfirmed, even with voice pre-fill. The first change to an ended, unconfirmed event confirms it too. Until then its marks stay listed as not confirmed (⏳) and don’t count toward rates or the no-show rule; the event’s own attendance panel still shows them, ready to confirm. Turn it off in /settings → Attendance rules to count every recorded event. It applies to past events too.

Attendance with extra characters

When a member brings extra characters, the attendance editor lists each character separately. Voice samples belong to the member and inform each character line, including the any-channel and late-leader rules. The no-show rule counts the member once per event: if one character attended, another character’s No-Show does not make that event a miss. Only all-No-Show lines make a missed event. Character lines hidden by the event’s rule remain hidden on ended records and attendance too. Marks for temporarily hidden extras stay stored when the host’s plan or Characters on events switch changes.

The current settings apply to past events too. Unanswered recurring-date confirmations still count as misses when Count only confirmed attendance is on.

The no-show rule

Off by default (/settings → Attendance rules). Members who missed more than a set share of their recent signups (no-shows in attendance, or not confirming a recurring date) wait on standby first, behind members who chose standby, and are called in at the standby fill time if places remain. Saying Can't make it in time isn't a miss, and the window moves on as members attend. /attendance shows where you stand; members who can manage the server can select a member in Attendance management and change their exemption.
