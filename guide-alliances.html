Alliances

Overview · Members · Organizers · Restricted events · Attendance · Roles · Alliances · Server admins

An alliance links your server with one you trust, so events can be shared between them; an invite code opens one event to any server. Alliance settings are in /settings → Community → Server Alliances (needs Manage Server, unless your server changed Settings access).

Forming · Shared roles · Role mapping · Who shares, and what you take · Sharing an event · Finding replacements · Invite codes · Cancelling

Forming an alliance

  • One server’s admin presses +Create an Alliance, enters the Roles to Share and chooses whether to accept shared events and replacement requests, then presses Create Code / Enter Code. The code (such as EM-7K3Q-9XVD-M2PA) is shown once, works once, and expires after 24 hours.
  • An admin of the other server opens the same Create flow, chooses their own roles and acceptance settings, then uses Enter their code…. Either server entering the other’s code forms the alliance for both; only one redemption is needed. The alliance forms straight away, and both servers’ admin channels and audit logs record it.
  • On the Free plan a server can have 2 allies; Paid has no limit.

Shared roles

What you share is what your members bring to the ally’s events. You can share any role the bot tracks here:

  • Discord roles the bot uses: in role groups, categories, roster slots, preset pings or statics.
  • Bot roles: categories without a Discord role of their own, such as Healer = WHM, SCH. A member holds one by having any of its roles, so sharing Healer alone tells the ally “this member heals” without listing their jobs.

Enter the tracked role names to share, one per line. You can share a named category itself, individual roles, or leave the entry empty to share no roles. Where a bot role and a Discord role have the same name, the bot role reads Healer (bot role). Change what you share later with Edit Settings → Roles to Share….

Role mapping

Each server maps its own shared roles onto the ally’s roles: what your members count as in their events. You know your roles best, so you say “our Healer is your Support”. The ally can override any line for its own events.

  • Edit Mappings → Your roles: their roles shows one line per role you share, Your role: their role, suggested by name when the alliance forms. Map onto any role the bot tracks in their server, Discord or bot role; leave a line blank (or delete it) and that role isn’t sent.
  • One of yours can be several of theirs: Healer: WHM, SCH means your Healers count there as both WHM and SCH, so they can fill either slot (and any category those are in). Several of yours can also be the same one of theirs.
  • Your categories do the composing: a member holds a bot role you share by having any of its roles. If you share Healer = WHM, SCH and map Healer: Support, your SCH player arrives in their events as Support.
  • Edit Mappings → Their roles: your roles shows what the ally’s roles count as in your events: their mapping, as they set it. Change a line to override it for your events (your override wins, and they see that you overrode it); delete a line to follow theirs again; leave it blank and that role counts as nothing here.
  • The bot keeps roles by ID underneath, so renaming a role keeps its mapping. A role that’s deleted on either side stops being shared or mapped; the forms then show what needs a new choice.

Edit Mappings appears when either server shares roles. Mappings are optional: roles can be left unmapped, and no mapping-completeness warning or Inbox alert is shown.

Who shares, and what you take

  • Who can share events and ask allies for players: admins (Manage Server, or the owner), plus any roles you choose in /settings → Community → Community Events (for example your raid leads). A Crosspost: line works only if the event’s creator is one of them. /crosspost-event and /find-replacements appear (in servers with allies) to members with Manage Server; Discord only lets server admins show a command to other roles, in Server Settings → Integrations → the bot, so allow your chosen roles there.
  • What you take from each ally: choose an ally, then Edit Settings → Accept Shared Events and Accept Replacement Requests: take both, one, or neither, then save. Turning one off takes down that ally’s posts of that kind in your server.
  • Sharing and replacements: add more allies to a shared event whenever needed. If the hosting server stops sharing, it can share again later. If a receiving server stops that event being shared there, the host cannot share that event to it again. Replacement searches still happen once per event date, to the allies chosen together.

Sharing an event (crossposts)

  • Put Crosspost: Moonfall FC (allies’ server names, separated by commas) in a Discord event’s description, run /crosspost-event in its thread, or press Manage on its roster → Community… and choose allies (see who can). You can add more allies later; allies already receiving this event are not offered again.
  • Stopping: removing the line, Stop sharing with, the alliance ending, or the ally no longer taking your shared events all stop it there. Its post says so and nobody else can sign up from that server. Its members who already have a roster place keep it (your organizers can still remove them); everyone else from there is signed out. Both groups are mentioned in their thread, which stays until the event’s start time and is then deleted.
  • The bot posts the event in each ally’s events channel, with a thread showing the same roster and Join, Change role and Leave. Their members sign up there as if they were in your server: your role mapping decides what they can fill, and your restrictions and blocks apply. They’re pinged in their own thread when the pre-event roster goes up.
  • Each thread shows its own server’s members as mentions with their names, and everyone else by name and server, such as Brynna (Moonfall FC). Each server’s own presets decide who’s pinged when the event is posted there.
  • Reminders reach every server: a reminder (/remind, from your thread or an ally’s) is posted in each ally’s thread too, pinging their roles that would fill the openings (by their mapping), or the pings of their own preset for the event.
  • Join requests: an ally’s member the roster can’t place (no mapped role fits an open slot) is listed under Incoming join requests instead of Invalid signups. Community… → Join requests lets the event’s organizers accept them into a role (they’re added at the front) or turn them down.
  • A recurring event stays shared for every date. When an event ends, the ally’s post and thread are deleted. If it’s cancelled, they say so and stay until its start time.
  • To stop receiving one shared event, open Manage on its shared roster and confirm Stop sharing this event here…. This stops future shares of that event to your server; other shared events and allies are unaffected. Removing only the calendar copy does not reject the shared event.
  • On the Free plan a server can crosspost one event a week (an event counts once, however many allies it goes to, and a recurring series is one event); Paid has no limit.
  • In your own calendar: an ally’s organizers (Manage Events) and admins can press Manage… on the shared roster in their thread → Add to our calendar. The bot makes a Discord event in their server, named and timed like yours, linking to the thread, and keeps it in step when you rename or reschedule. Interested on it does what it does for that server’s own events: signs members up, or puts them on standby, or (Join-only servers) just follows it. It’s removed when the event ends, is cancelled, or stops being shared there, and a recurring event’s copy moves on to each next date. Remove from our calendar takes it out (signups stay). The bot needs Create Events in that server. Free.

Characters on shared events

On a Paid home server with characters on, make characters with /character in your own server. When the host is Paid with characters on, Join on a shared roster offers those characters and Myself (all roles), then the role question if the host asks it. A character’s roles that you still hold follow the host’s role mapping. The role question offers what those mapped roles fit, plus Any role I have and Standby. Restrictions check your own mapped roles; a character cannot get around them.

In your own server, the line uses the character’s name with your mention. Other servers see a label such as Aldric (Brynna, Moonfall FC) — Tank. Your character’s name reaches the hosting server and every other server the event is shared with. Editing or deleting the character in your own server updates active shared rosters too; deleting it falls back to Myself.

The hosting server’s plan and switch decide for local and allied members. On Free or Characters on events: Only Myself, active character signups read as Myself (all roles) and extra lines collapse into that line, without pings. Characters, their roles and their roster names are ignored. Nothing is deleted; restoring Paid and As many as you want before the event restores lines in their original order. Ended events keep recorded names. Leave and Manage can remove hidden lines. Attendance stays per line, with one member outcome per event.

Finding replacements

  • Short of players? Run /find-replacements in the event’s thread, or press Manage → Community…, and choose allies (all that can be asked are chosen to start with; see who can). Each event date asks once.
  • Replacements or sharing? Sharing an event gives an ally the whole roster to sign up on; finding replacements only asks for the roles you still need, and your organizers choose who comes. An ally the event is already shared with isn’t asked (it has the event already).
  • Each ally gets a post in its events channel, like a reminder: when the event starts, how many players you need and in which roles, who to contact (whoever asked, if they’re on the roster, else the player in slot #1, by name and Discord username), with an Apply button. It updates as places fill, and says the roster is full (thanks for the interest) once it is.
  • Members who press Apply choose the role they’d come as, from the open roles their mapped roles can fill. Each application is announced in your event’s thread (mentioning whoever asked), with a Review application button that opens it for the event’s organizers; they’re also under Community… → Applications. Accepting asks who should they contact? (filled in as above) and for an optional note, then adds them at the front in that role and tells them in the post’s thread, with the contact and note; turning one down just closes it.
  • The post and its thread are deleted when the event ends, and applications still waiting close with it. On the Free plan a server can search for replacements once a week; Paid has no limit.

Invite codes for one event (Paid)

  • To open one event to servers you aren’t allied with, press Manage → Community… → Make an invite code (see who can). The code (such as EV-7K3Q-9XVD-M2PA) is shown once and works for any number of servers until the event (or, for a recurring event, that date) ends; making a new one replaces it.
  • An organizer or admin of another server enters it in /settings → Community → Community Events → Enter an Event Invite, or with /community-event (shown to members with Manage Events). The event is posted in their events channel, like a replacement search, with an Apply button.
  • Their members apply choosing any of the roles they could fill (named as on your roster) and, if they like, a note. Your organizers decide under Community… → Applications, as for replacements.

Cancelling

Dissolve ends it for both servers, and both admin channels are told. Shared events are taken down on both sides; players already placed on an upcoming roster keep their place. An alliance also ends if the bot is removed from either server. You can ally again later with a new code.
