Signing up

  • Join: press Join on the roster in the event’s thread. What clicking Interested on the Discord event does depends on the server: it signs you up too, puts you on standby until you press Join, or just follows the event (you’re told if its time changes). The bot can’t mark you Interested, so after Join it links to the event in case you want Discord’s own reminder. Your place in line is the time the bot first saw you sign up; if Interested only put you on standby or following, your place counts from pressing Join.
  • Leave: remove Interested, or press Leave. If you come back within the server’s grace period (5 minutes unless changed), you keep your original place. While your place is held, Leave offers Withdraw now to give it up straight away, also after removing Interested.
  • Your role: by default you’re matched on the roles you already have. Change role lets you pick a specific role you’re bringing, “Any role I have”, or Standby. If the event has an Open slot, it also offers the server’s listed Open slot roles; the roster shows a choice such as BRD (Open).
  • Preferences: in /whoami you can say which roles you prefer, and which you’d rather not play. The bot uses them when placing you on rosters, but they never cost you your place, and choosing a role for one event always wins. Filling the event's named role slots comes first: if you're the only one who can fill one, you're placed there even if you prefer another role. A flex slot (shown as “RDM (flex)”) takes its role first; anyone can stand in until someone with the role signs up. If that happens before the pre-event roster, the stand-in is told once, stays signed up and is first in line for the next place.
  • Role fit: the last step of /whoami is for a hard no: pick a preset (such as Odyssey) and the roles you’re never to be placed as in its events. You keep the role and its pings; choosing a role for one event, or an organizer placing you, still wins.
  • Standby: you wait outside the roster and are moved into an open slot shortly before the event (an hour by default), or earlier if an organizer fills from standby. You get a mention when that happens.

Your characters (Paid)

On Paid with characters on, use /character to create, edit or delete characters in this server. Give each one a name and choose from the roles you hold that the bot uses. You can save up to 24 characters per member per server; the picker has pages when needed. After the name, Create… and Edit… ask you to pick the roles the character plays, then Prefer and Rather not, then roles to never be placed as in each preset’s events. Preferences and never-placed choices only offer that character’s own roles; Edit starts with its saved choices. Prefer and Rather not never cost a place; never-placed is a hard no for that preset. Each character keeps its own choices, separate from your /whoami nickname and preferences. Existing characters start with none.

When you have saved characters, Join asks who you’re bringing: a character or Myself (all roles). Myself uses all your roles and your /whoami preferences. Interested never chooses a character or adds an extra. If Join asks for a role too, that question comes next. Choosing a specific role for the event wins; Any role I have uses the character’s roles that you still hold. In your own server you can still choose a role you don’t hold.

Change role → Change character switches your character without changing your chosen role for the event, including after the pre-event roster. Editing a character updates its active events without new pings. Deleting it returns linked signups to Myself; ended-event names follow the normal retention and privacy rules. Losing a Discord role stops it qualifying the character until you regain it, but does not remove it from the saved character. Restrictions and Static membership always check you, not the character.

The roster shows Aldric (@Brynna) — Tank for a character, or Brynna (@Brynna) — Tank for Myself (only (@Brynna) when no display name is available). Organizers can still choose the role you bring or pin a place. For allied events, choose a character saved in your own server; its name is shared with the hosting server and every server receiving that event. Its Prefer and Rather not choices apply through the host’s role mappings; never-placed choices belong to your home server’s presets and do not apply to allied events.

In /character, choose a character and press ★ Main to make it your main in this server. Starring another moves the star; deleting your main leaves none. Your main appears with a star on your character list.

Events follow the host’s current character default unless the organizer sets an event rule. No event limit allows extra saved characters where characters are on. An event can use Main only (Join brings your main with no choice to make, or Myself if you haven’t starred one), One character each (any character or Myself, with no extras), or Extras last. With Extras last, until the event starts, a waiting first character takes the place of the latest-added extra that can give them a place, even after the pre-event roster. The first character is told as usual; the extra moves to standby without a ping. The host’s rule applies to allied signups; your main is the one saved in your own server.

If a limit is added after signups, signups on a Main only event show the member’s main (or Myself without one) and extras on Main only or One character each are hidden. Nothing is deleted and no one is pinged; lifting the limit before the event brings those lines back. Free and Only Myself still use Myself.

Bringing more than one character (Paid)

On a Paid host with Characters on events: As many as you want, Join or Change role offers Bring another character after joining with a character. Choose any unused saved character, never Myself. The switch starts on. There is no event cap beyond your saved characters.

Each character takes its own roster place and uses its own roles and preferences. Extras wait behind everyone’s first character, oldest extra first; a roster ping mentions you once. Choose the character when changing its role or leaving, or choose All characters to leave them all. An individual extra leaves immediately; the original signup keeps the server’s usual grace period. Extra characters are for this event date; Interested does not add them to later dates.

On Free or Characters on events: Only Myself, active character signups read as Myself (all roles) and extra lines collapse into that line, without pings. Characters, their roles, preferences and roster names are ignored. Nothing is deleted; restoring Paid and As many as you want before the event restores lines in their original order. Ended events keep recorded names. Leave and Manage can remove hidden lines. Attendance stays per line, with one member outcome per event.

Both /privacy → Redact identity and Delete my data delete your saved characters in every server this bot instance serves. Character names are cleared from attendance and hidden in older roster posts; Resume does not restore deleted characters. See Privacy.

The roster

  • The roster appears in the event’s thread with the first signup and updates as people join and leave. First characters take priority over extras, even when they join later. Among first characters, earlier eligible signups keep priority. Open slots are listed with the role each needs.
  • If an event needs particular roles, players are placed into slots that fit them; anyone who can’t fit is listed under Invalid signups with the reason.
  • If someone withdraws from a full roster, the next eligible player is promoted and a short notice says who replaced whom.
  • Players without a character are shown as a mention with their server name after it; characters show their name with the member’s mention. Edits to the roster never notify anyone; shortly before the start (15 minutes by default) the bot posts a fresh Pre-event roster, which does notify the players placed on it.
  • If the start time changes, everyone signed up is mentioned with the old and new times.
  • When the event ends, its thread is archived: it disappears from the channel list but its history stays.

Your static

A static’s card shows its members and a welcome explaining who to contact and how to plan its events. Look for it in the channel’s pinned messages; if someone unpins it, the bot leaves that choice alone. Leaders use Manage, then choose Welcome message… to write their own welcome. Leaving it blank restores the default, and mentions in it never ping.

Statics are set up by admins. Ask an admin to create one, rename it or dissolve it. Existing statics keep working. If you lead one, open Manage on its card or /static. The dropdown offers Members to add or remove people and Welcome message… to edit the welcome. On Paid, an admin can create private text and voice channels with a new static. See Statics for the details.

Static events normally appear in the static’s text channel, or the channel holding its card. If someone opens enrollment for a date, follow the old thread’s link to its new public thread in the announcement channel. The next date returns to the static’s channel and is restricted again.
