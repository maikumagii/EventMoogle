For event organizers

Overview · Members · Organizers · Restricted events · Attendance · Roles · Alliances · Server admins

The person who created a Discord event, and server administrators, can organize it. Organizer tools are in the roster’s Manage button (or /roster manage) and the /roster command, used inside the event’s thread. Restricting who can sign up has its own page, as does attendance.

Choosing the roster

  • Presets are rosters the server has saved, such as “Raid: 2 Tanks, 2 Healers, 4 DPS” or “Scrim: 5 players”. An event whose name contains a preset’s keyword gets that preset automatically; otherwise it gets the server’s default roster. Keywords match whole words and their plurals (raid matches “Raids”), and mage + sortie needs both words in any order (“Sortie with Mages”). Presets are in an order the admins set: the first one with a matching keyword wins. Until someone other than you signs up (Discord marks you Interested when you create the event), renaming the event picks the matching preset again.
  • After that, renaming the event to match a different preset posts an Apply / Keep current prompt for you.
  • /roster preset (or Manage → Apply a preset) applies any preset by hand.
  • /roster expand adds a party, copied from the preset’s repeatable party and adjusted in a form; /roster shrink removes the last party.
  • /roster slots and /roster role set this event’s own size and role slots.

Arranging players

Press Manage on the roster (or run /roster manage in the event’s thread). The private panel does it all:

  • Choose a player from the list, then Move… (pin them into a party; Paid), Swap… (with another player; Paid: Swap parties moves each to the other’s party, into a slot they fit; Swap places gives each the other’s exact slot and its role, and keeps them there), Unpin, Remove (exclude them, even if Interested) or Set role… (the role they’re bringing). Your placements win over a member’s role fit.
  • Add a player puts someone at the front of the signups.
  • Slots… sets this event’s size and role slots; apply a preset; add or remove a party; Fill from standby now; Attendance… (Paid).

/roster has manage, attendance and export (a CSV of the roster; Paid). With Expanded commands on (/settings → General), it also has typed versions of every panel action: move, swap, unpin, add, remove, set-role, fill, slots, role, preset, expand and shrink. The panel lists the first 25 signups; for bigger events, use the typed commands.

Setting up from the event description

Write a block like this in the Discord event’s description:

Roster: @Aldric, Brynna, Corvin
Open slots: 2
Slots: Healer, 2x Tank
  • Listed players (mentions, or names that match exactly one member) take the first slots and are signed up for you. In servers that sign up only through Discord’s Interested button, their places are reserved until they click Interested (not for anyone who said they can’t make it).
  • Open slots adds slots anyone can fill; Slots adds slots for roles or categories by name (Open for an open one).
  • Edit the description and the roster follows, until someone who isn’t listed (other than you, the creator) signs up. Names or roles the bot can’t find are listed in the thread.
  • Other lines in the description: Restricted: and Private: (see Restricted events), Voice: required (see Attendance), Crosspost: (see Alliances).

Cancelled events

Cancel (or delete) an event in Discord before it starts and the bot doesn’t just tidy it away: the thread says This event was cancelled and mentions everyone signed up, the roster loses its buttons, and the announcement says it was cancelled. Allied servers’ posts of it say so too, mentioning their members. Everything stays until the time the event was due to start, so nobody misses the news; then the thread is archived as usual.

Recurring events

Each date of a repeating Discord event gets its own post, thread and roster. When a date ends, its thread is archived and the next date is posted. Members who are Interested in the series carry over to every date, keeping their place in line; Not interested on one date leaves them out of that date only. Join counts for one date: mark Interested to stay on the series.

Confirm Attendance

On by default (/settings → Recurring events). Members Interested in the series keep a reserved place on each date and confirm it:

  • In Sign up mode they press Join (and Leave means “not this date”: they stay on the series); in Everything mode they press Confirm or Can't make it. In Standby and Follow only modes, pressing Join for each date already is the confirmation.
  • The roster shows ✅ for confirmed and ⏳ for waiting. One reminder pings those who haven't answered (2 hours before the deadline by default).
  • At the deadline (4 hours before the start by default), anyone who hasn't confirmed moves aside and standbys take their places; confirming later puts them back on standby.
  • Joining, or marking Interested on that date, counts as confirming; Discord's Not interested on that date counts as Can't make it. If the date is rescheduled or started early, the answers move with it.
  • On Paid, /attendance lookup shows how often each member confirmed, declined or didn't answer.
