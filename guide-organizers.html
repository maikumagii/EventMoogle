For event organizers

Overview · Members · Organizers · Restricted events · Attendance · Roles · Alliances · Server admins

The person who created a Discord event, and server administrators, can organize it. Organizer tools are in the roster’s Manage button in the event’s thread. Restricting who can sign up has its own page, as does attendance.

Choosing the roster

  • Presets are rosters the server has saved, such as “Raid: 2 Tanks, 2 Healers, 4 DPS” or “Scrim: 5 players”. An event whose name contains a preset’s keyword gets that preset automatically; otherwise it gets the server’s default roster. Keywords match whole words and their plurals (raid matches “Raids”), and mage + sortie needs both words in any order (“Sortie with Mages”). Presets are in an order the admins set: the first one with a matching keyword wins.
  • Events keep up with their preset: renaming the event picks the matching preset again, and when an admin edits the preset, its roster or its notifications, the event updates. Signups and their order stay, nothing is posted again and nobody is pinged again.
  • Manage → Party Composition → Apply a preset applies any preset by hand, including ✋ manual ones. It stays applied until the event is renamed.
  • Manage → Party Composition → Edit Composition… builds this event’s own parties and slots from pickers: add or remove slots by role or category, add a party (a copy of the one that repeats) or remove one, then Save. That roster stays this event’s own: preset edits no longer change it. On a repeating event it applies to that date only, and the bot offers to turn it into a preset.

Finding a static’s event

With a Static preset and no typed Channel, new events go to the static’s text channel, otherwise its card’s channel, otherwise the announcement channel. A typed Channel takes priority; if the bot cannot use the destination, it falls back to the announcement channel and Status explains why. Existing posts stay where they are when a preset or card channel changes. The static’s welcome can tell you which keyword to put in a Discord event’s name.

Open enrollment on an event posted in the static’s channel asks to move this date to the announcement channel with a new public thread. The old thread links there and is locked but stays readable. The next date returns to the static’s channel, restricted again. See Open enrollment.

Arranging players

Press Manage on the roster. The private panel does it all in one message, like /settings: each tool opens in place with Back to Manage (Community’s sharing, replacements, join requests and applications too), and an action's result shows at the top of the menu so you can carry on. It closes after five minutes without use; press Manage again to reopen it.

  • Choose Add a Player, Remove a Player, Move/Swap a Player or Pin a Player's Role from the dropdown, then select the player.
  • Add a Player puts them at the front of the queue, including someone previously on standby. Remove a Player excludes them from this event; they cannot sign back up unless an organizer adds them again.
  • Move/Swap a Player offers Move… (pin them into another party; Paid), Swap… (Paid) and Unpin (restore automatic placement). Swap parties keeps each player's role fit; Swap places gives each the other's exact slot and its role.
  • Pin a Player's Role specifies the role they are bringing without changing signup priority.
  • The bottom row has Fill from Standby, Party Composition and Community…. Party Composition holds this event's slots, preset and party tools; Community holds sharing, replacements and invite codes.

Use /attendance in an event’s thread to open that event’s attendance if you can mark it, during or after the event. Other members get their own record with that event first. Attendance is also on the pre-event roster when enabled. Organizer tools use the private panel; there is no /roster command or Export roster action in Manage.

New event threads combine the introduction, its notification mentions and initial roster in one card. Later roster edits do not repeat the notification. The pre-event roster still notifies its selected players when configured. Existing separate posts keep their IDs; unusually long introductions and rosters remain separate to retain all text.

Characters on your roster

Members use /character and choose who they bring on Join. A character shows as Aldric (@Brynna) — Tank; an allied character shows as Aldric (Brynna, Moonfall FC) — Tank outside its own server. Matching uses that character’s roles the member still holds, unless a role was chosen for the event. Restrictions and Static membership check the member’s own roles. Pinning or changing a role keeps the character’s name; no pin symbol is added to the line.

On Paid, Characters on events: As many as you want in Settings → Events → General lets members choose characters and bring more than one. It is on by default; Only Myself turns characters off. Each character takes a place; first characters precede extras, which keep their added order. Manage edits each line separately. Pings mention each member once. The hosting server’s plan and switch apply to allies.

On Free or Characters on events: Only Myself, active character signups read as Myself (all roles) and extra lines collapse into that line, without pings. Characters, their roles and their roster names are ignored. Nothing is deleted; restoring Paid and As many as you want before the event restores lines in their original order. Ended events keep recorded names. Leave and Manage can remove hidden lines. Attendance stays per line, with one member outcome per event.

When an event has an Open slot, Join and Change role offer the roles in the groups admins chose under What roles are in an Open slot? in Events → General. A chosen role reads BRD (Open) in an Open slot; leaving the choice to the bot keeps the usual Open label.

Setting up from the event description

Write a block like this in the Discord event’s description:

Roster: @Aldric, Brynna, Corvin
Open slots: 2
Slots: Healer, 2x Tank
  • Listed players (mentions, or names that match exactly one member) take the first slots and are signed up for you. In servers that sign up only through Discord’s Interested button, their places are reserved until they click Interested (not for anyone who said they can’t make it).
  • Open slots adds slots anyone can fill; Slots adds slots for roles or categories by name (Open for an open one).
  • Edit the description and the roster follows, until someone who isn’t listed (other than you, the creator) signs up. Names or roles the bot can’t find are listed in the thread.
  • Other lines in the description: Restricted: and Private: (see Restricted events), Voice: required (see Attendance), Crosspost: (see Alliances).

Cancelled events

Cancel (or delete) an event in Discord before it starts and the bot doesn’t just tidy it away: the thread says This event was cancelled and mentions everyone signed up, the roster loses its buttons, and the announcement says it was cancelled. Allied servers’ posts of it say so too, mentioning their members. Everything stays until the time the event was due to start, so nobody misses the news; then the thread is archived as usual.

Recurring events

Each date of a repeating Discord event gets its own post, thread and roster. When a date ends, its thread is archived and the next date is posted. Members who are Interested in the series carry over to every date, keeping their place in line; Not interested on one date leaves them out of that date only. Join counts for one date: mark Interested to stay on the series.

Confirm Attendance

On by default (/settings → Recurring events). Members Interested in the series keep a reserved place on each date and confirm it:

  • In Sign up mode they press Join (and Leave means “not this date”: they stay on the series); in Interested only mode they press Confirm or Can't make it. In Standby and Follow only modes, pressing Join for each date already is the confirmation.
  • The roster shows ✅ for confirmed and ⏳ for waiting. One reminder pings those who haven't answered (2 hours before the deadline by default).
  • At the deadline (4 hours before the start by default), anyone who hasn't confirmed moves aside and standbys take their places; confirming later puts them back on standby.
  • Joining, or marking Interested on that date, counts as confirming; Discord's Not interested on that date counts as Can't make it. If the date is rescheduled or started early, the answers move with it.
  • On Paid, /attendance opens Attendance management; select a member to see how often they confirmed, declined or didn't answer.
