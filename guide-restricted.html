Restricted events and statics

Overview · Members · Organizers · Restricted events · Attendance · Roles · Alliances · Server admins

Restricting who can sign up

Only members with certain roles can sign up: put Restricted: Static, Officers (role or category names) in a Discord event’s description, or set Who can sign up on a preset in /settings → Presets. The description line wins over the preset, and Restricted: no lets anyone sign up for that one event. Pinging a role (or a static) never restricts who can sign up.

Static presets: choose Static… on a preset to make it one static’s events. Its members are added to each roster and asked to confirm, only they can sign up, the static is pinged, and the thread follows the server’s private-thread setting (see below). Open enrollment opens one date to everyone without showing the private thread’s history. A preset has one static at most; Who can sign up applies only to presets without one.

  • Others who press Join get a private reply naming the roles; their Interested clicks don’t put them on the roster. Members who gain a role appear straight away.
  • Members already signed up when a restriction is added stay, marked 🔒 on the roster; remove them by hand if needed. Organizers can always add anyone.
  • Pings: a restricted event pings only the roles it’s restricted to (for a Static preset, the static), in place of the preset’s usual pings. A private event without a restriction pings nobody. Once enrollment is open on a public event, reminders ping as usual for that date.

Private threads

  • Restricted events get a private thread by default (/settings → Statics → Restricted events private turns that off). Private: no in the description keeps one event public, and Private: yes makes any event private. Either line counts only before the bot posts the event, since a thread can’t change type later.
  • The bot adds the restricted roles, the event’s creator, and everyone who signs up. It needs Create Private Threads in the channel; without it, the event shows an error rather than a public thread.

Open enrollment

  • The event’s thread has an Open enrollment button. Any member with one of the roles (or the organizer) can press it to let anyone sign up for this date; it then disappears.
  • For a static’s event posted in the static’s channel, the button asks first, then posts this date in the server’s announcement channel with a new public thread. This happens even if the original thread is public: a thread inside a private channel still cannot be seen by everyone. The old thread says where the event went and is locked but stays readable to people who could already see it.
  • On other private events, Discord can’t make the thread public, so opening enrollment asks first and creates a new public thread. The bot needs View Channel, Send Messages and Create Public Threads in the announcement channel; if it cannot post there, enrollment stays closed.
  • On a recurring event the next date is restricted again, returns to the static’s channel when it has one, and follows the private-thread setting. Members without the role who were only let in for one date leave its roster.

Statics

A static is a fixed group, such as a raid static. EventMoogle stores its members in the database, so Restricted: Raid Static works even without a Discord role. Admins create statics in /settings → Statics. Members who want a static ask an admin. With Role management enabled, new statics also get a role with no permissions. Existing linked-role members are imported from a complete Discord member cache; later manual Discord role changes stay in sync. Incomplete imports remain pending.

Turning Role management off preserves the group and its Discord role; membership controls still edit the group but stop changing Discord roles. Deleting a linked role preserves the group and its restrictions. Group pings use the role when its complete member list exactly matches the group and that saves pings, otherwise they mention members individually.

  • Admins manage statics in /settings → Statics: Create static… names it; add and remove members; the Static settings menu offers Members, Welcome message…, Name and purpose…, Leader and card and Dissolve…; Dissolve… removes the group and, with Role management enabled, takes its linked role from members or deletes it.
  • Leaders add and remove members by right-clicking a member → Apps → Add to my static or Remove from my static (asked which, if they lead several), with Manage → Members on the static’s card, or with /static. These tools also let leaders edit the welcome; they do not rename or dissolve the group.
  • The card: a post in a channel admins choose, showing its leader and members, followed by a welcome. It updates as members come and go. When a static gets a new private text channel, that is its default card channel.

Welcome message

The default welcome names the leader to contact and explains how to plan an event for the static. When an automatic preset uses the static, it gives that preset’s keyword; otherwise it explains the Restricted: description line. Without a leader, it asks readers to contact an admin.

Leaders open Manage on the card or /static, then choose Welcome message…; admins use Static settings → Welcome message… in /settings → Statics. Write up to 1,000 characters, such as your schedule or group rules. Discord formatting, links and emoji work; mentions show without pinging anyone. The form shows your saved custom welcome, or is blank when the default is in use. Leave it blank to restore the current default. Welcomes are on Free and Paid.

Pinned cards

EventMoogle pins a card when it posts or moves it. Existing cards get one pin attempt after the v3.3 upgrade; later edits do not pin them again, so a manual unpin stays. There is no pinning switch. The bot needs Pin Messages in the card’s channel. If permission is missing, or Discord’s pin limit is reached, the card is posted unpinned and Status explains why. The bot never unpins someone else’s message to make room. Moving a card unpins and removes the old post; dissolving removes its card too.

Where static events are posted

On a preset with a Static, leave Channel blank to use the static’s own text channel, otherwise the channel holding its card, otherwise the server’s announcement channel. The form’s hint shows the static’s channel when it has one. A typed Channel takes priority. Using a card’s channel works on Free as well as Paid.

The bot needs View Channel, Send Messages and Create Private Threads or Create Public Threads, according to the thread being created. A missing or unsuitable destination falls back to the announcement channel and is explained in Status. Changing the destination affects new posts; already-posted events stay where they are unless enrollment is opened. Events shared with allies still use each ally’s event feed.

Private text and voice channels (Paid)

The admin’s Create static… form in Settings → Statics offers Private text and voice channels when Role management is on and the bot has Manage Channels. The static gets its own role. Choose Create text and voice channels to make them; No channels is the default. This choice is absent on Free, while Role management is off, or without Manage Channels, and is not offered when editing an existing static.

Admins choose Statics category in /settings → Statics; clear it to create channels at the top level. It applies to new channels. A static named Ironclad gets #ironclad and a voice channel named Ironclad. Only its role, the bot and people with Administrator can see them; its role can connect and speak in voice. Renaming the static renames both channels while channel automation is available. If someone deletes a channel, the bot forgets it, reports it in Status and does not recreate it.

Dissolve… asks whether to keep or delete a static’s channels in the same confirmation. Keep channels for an admin to remove is the default; Delete channels and their history loses their history. Kept channels are left for an admin to remove. If Paid ends, creation and automatic renaming pause, while existing channels and their saved IDs stay. Explicit deletion on dissolve is still allowed on Free. Turning Role management off also pauses channel automation.
