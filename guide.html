Event Moogle User Guide

Event Moogle turns Discord scheduled events into signup lists and rosters. Members sign up by clicking Interested on an event or Join on its roster; the bot keeps everyone in the order they signed up, fills the roster’s slots, and keeps a thread for each event up to date. This page lists what it does; each part has its own page with the details.

Overview · Members · Organizers · Restricted events · Attendance · Roles · Alliances · Server admins

For members

  • Sign up with Join on the roster, or Interested on the event; leave with Leave, keeping your place if you come back within a few minutes.
  • Choose your role, or stand by for a place that opens shortly before the event.
  • Your profile with /whoami: nickname, roles, pings, roles you prefer or would rather not play, and roles you never play in some events.
  • The roster fills slots in signup order, promotes the next player when someone leaves, and posts a pre-event roster that mentions everyone on it.

For event organizers

  • Presets give each event its roster from keywords in its name; parties, role slots and open slots.
  • Manage on the roster: move, swap, pin, add or remove players, set slots, fill from standby, take attendance.
  • Description setup: list players and slots in the Discord event’s description.
  • Recurring events: a fresh roster per date, with Confirm Attendance so places go to people who are coming.

Restricted events

  • Only certain roles can sign up, from a Restricted: line or a preset; private threads by default.
  • Open enrollment lets anyone in for one date; statics are fixed groups with optional Discord roles, made by admins or by members with the static button.

Attendance (Paid)

  • Voice presence pre-fills an attendance check organizers confirm; history, rates and CSV export.
  • Fair counting: half attendance on voice-required events, late arrivals, voiding an event, and an optional no-show rule that puts members who often miss on standby first.

Roles

  • Categories such as Healer = Cleric, Druid, with or without a role of their own.
  • Self-assignable roles in /whoami, and button posts in your roles channel with emoji, colours and names.
  • Role management is an opt-in module for self-roles, category sync and linked static roles, with setup checks and permission alerts.

Alliances

  • Link with a server you trust using a one-time code, choosing the roles you share (Discord roles or bot roles such as Healer), and map them onto the ally’s roles (the ally can override for its events).
  • Share an event with allies, once: one roster, signed up from either server; members who don’t fit become join requests.
  • Find replacements: ask allies for the players you still need; their members apply and your organizers choose.
  • You decide who can share and ask, and whether you take each ally’s shared events, replacement searches, or both.
  • Invite codes (Paid): open one event to any server; its members apply.

For server admins

  • /setup walks through the basics; /settings has everything, section by section.
  • Notifications (ping rules), timings and message templates.
  • Safety: elevated roles need your confirmation, member blocks, spam pauses, an admin channel for alerts, and who can open /settings.
  • Audit log (Paid): who did what, and when.

Free and Paid

Free is the complete bot: signups, rosters with parties, buttons, standby, reminders, presets (the first two in your order apply automatically by keyword, the rest by hand), description setup, restricted events, every message template, 10 ping rules, and 15 events maintained at a time.

Paid, a server subscription bought in Discord, adds automation and reporting: every preset applied by keyword, automatic category roles, moving and swapping players, attendance with history and stats, the audit log, CSV export, 25 ping rules, 50 events at a time, and priority when the bot is busy. If a subscription ends, nothing is deleted; Paid features pause until you subscribe again.

Privacy

  • /privacy → Redact identity hides your name and mentions in the bot’s posts, everywhere. Your signups through Discord’s Interested keep working (shown as <redacted upon request> in their place), and you can always leave; the bot’s own sign-up buttons and everything across servers (signing up or applying through an allied server, Visitor invites) are off for you, since they’d only send a nameless entry.
  • /privacy → Delete my data deletes your signups and attendance everywhere and stops the bot tracking you. Signing up again yourself turns tracking back on. /privacy → Resume undoes both redact and delete from then on: the bot tracks you and shows your name again (deleted data and posts already redacted stay as they are).
  • Data about ended events is deleted automatically after 90 days; attendance records are kept. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Words used here

  • Slot: one place on a roster, either for a role (Tank, Healer) or open to anyone.
  • Party: a group of slots; a roster can have several, up to 25 slots in total.
  • Category: a name (with or without a role of its own) that covers several roles, such as Healer covering Cleric and Druid.
  • Preset: a saved roster the server can reuse, picked by keywords in event names.
  • Ping rule: keywords that make a new event mention certain roles.
  • Standby: a signup that waits outside the roster until shortly before the event.
