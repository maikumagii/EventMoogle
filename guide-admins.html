Getting started

When the bot is added, it posts a short welcome in the server’s system channel. Commands appear as they become useful: the event commands once there’s an announcement channel, /whoami once there’s something to pick. New to the bot? Run /setup (needs Manage Server). It starts by explaining how the bot works (it reads your Discord events, picks a preset by keywords in each event's name, uses that preset's party composition, then fills the slots by matchmaking signups), then walks through where events are posted, roles, categories and presets, in that order. Continue later keeps your place; /setup stays until you press Finish on the last step. /about-new-features (needs Manage Server) shows what’s new, page by page: the updates your admins haven’t read yet, otherwise the latest one.

/settings

Run /settings (needs Manage Server). It opens a private panel. Choose an area from the Home dropdown. Server opens Status, with Subscription, Sync Now and Run Setup Again buttons. Access & Safety has a dropdown for Settings Access, Safety, Admin Channel and Audit Log. Inbox stays available in the footer throughout.

Static cards and channels

Choose a static in /settings → Statics. The Static settings dropdown offers Members, Welcome message…, Name and purpose…, Leader and card and Dissolve…. Use Name and purpose to rename the static or change which events it is for; signup restrictions still come from the preset’s Static. Leader and card chooses who leads it and where its card goes. A blank welcome restores the default. Cards show the welcome below their members and never ping on edits. Pinning needs Pin Messages: Status reports a refused pin, including Discord’s pin limit, without removing any other pin.

On Paid, Create static… can also create private text and voice channels when Role management is on and the bot can Manage Channels. Members ask an admin to create a static; existing statics and their leaders keep working. Choose a Statics category, or clear the picker for top level. Existing statics do not gain channels automatically. The static’s role and the bot have access; admins see them through Administrator. Dissolve… keeps channels by default and asks before deleting their history. Channel creation, renaming, deletion and category changes are recorded in the Paid audit log. See Private text and voice channels for plan lapses and deleted channels.

Safety

  • Elevated roles: a self-assignable role (or ping role) that grants permissions @everyone doesn’t have is offered to members only after you confirm it: Review elevated roles shows each one with the permissions it would give, to Confirm or Skip. If its permissions change later, it stops being offered until you review it again. Roles with Administrator are never offered.
  • Blocking: block members from giving themselves roles, or from signing up for events (organizers can still add them; anyone can always leave), or choose a blocking role. The two member pickers show existing blocks; selecting or unselecting members saves immediately. Unchecking removes only that restriction, preserving their other restriction and members on other pages. Full pages offer another page for additions.
  • Members who change roles, or press Join/Leave, too fast are paused for 10 minutes. The new-member wait was removed in v3.1.

Admin channel

Where security alerts are posted, on every plan (the bot checks it can post there; Send test message confirms it): spam pauses, blocked members trying anyway, elevated roles confirmed or changed, and privacy requests that hide someone’s admin actions. Problems that stop a preset or event working are posted there too: one message per change, listing every preset it affected, mentioning the person who made the change once (only when the bot knows for certain who it was). A problem that comes back is posted without a mention, and ✅ Resolved notices never ping. On Paid, you can also copy chosen audit log groups there. Keep it private to admins.

Settings access

Who can use /settings and /setup: members with Manage Server (the default), the server owner only, or the owner and roles you choose. Only the owner can change this. The roles picker appears only when the owner-and-chosen-roles option is selected. Organizers keep their event tools whatever it says.

Audit log (Paid)

Who did what, and when: settings changes, roster edits, attendance, every signup, members joining and leaving, and what the bot did with its permissions (roles, nicknames, channels and threads), grouped by the permission each needs. Filter by group, member and period, or Download CSV. Entries are kept for the retention period. If someone asks the bot to delete or redact their data, their actions as an organizer or admin stay, shown as <redacted upon request #0042>; for 72 hours a line in the log says who made the request.
