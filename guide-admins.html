Getting started

When the bot is added, it posts a short welcome in the server’s system channel. Commands appear as they become useful: the event commands once there’s an announcement channel, /whoami once there’s something to pick. New to the bot? Run /setup (needs Manage Server): it walks through the basics step by step and disappears once you finish. /about-new-features (needs Manage Server) shows what’s new, page by page: the updates your admins haven’t read yet, otherwise the latest one.

/settings

Run /settings (needs Manage Server). It opens a private panel. Choose an area from the Home dropdown. Server opens Status, with Subscription, Sync Now and Run Setup Again buttons. Access & Safety has a dropdown for Settings Access, Safety, Admin Channel and Audit Log. Inbox stays available in the footer throughout.

Safety

  • Elevated roles: a self-assignable role (or ping role) that grants permissions @everyone doesn’t have is offered to members only after you confirm it: Review elevated roles shows each one with the permissions it would give, to Confirm or Skip. If its permissions change later, it stops being offered until you review it again. Roles with Administrator are never offered.
  • Blocking: block members from giving themselves roles, or from signing up for events (organizers can still add them; anyone can always leave), or choose a blocking role. The two member pickers show existing blocks; selecting or unselecting members saves immediately. Unchecking removes only that restriction, preserving their other restriction and members on other pages. Full pages offer another page for additions.
  • Members who change roles, or press Join/Leave, too fast are paused for 10 minutes. The new-member wait was removed in v3.1.

Admin channel

Where security alerts are posted, on every plan (the bot checks it can post there; Send test message confirms it): spam pauses, blocked members trying anyway, elevated roles confirmed or changed, and privacy requests that hide someone’s admin actions. On Paid, you can also copy chosen audit log groups there. Keep it private to admins.

Settings access

Who can use /settings and /setup: members with Manage Server (the default), the server owner only, or the owner and roles you choose. Only the owner can change this. The roles picker appears only when the owner-and-chosen-roles option is selected. Organizers keep their event tools whatever it says.

Audit log (Paid)

Who did what, and when: settings changes, roster edits, attendance, every signup, members joining and leaving, and what the bot did with its permissions (roles, nicknames, threads), grouped by the permission each needs. Filter by group, member and period, or Download CSV. Entries are kept for the retention period. If someone asks the bot to delete or redact their data, their actions as an organizer or admin stay, shown as <redacted upon request #0042>; for 72 hours a line in the log says who made the request.
